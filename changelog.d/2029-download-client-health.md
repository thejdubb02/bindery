### Fixed
- **Download client health was only ever checked for qBittorrent** (#2029), so for SABnzbd, NZBGet, Transmission, Deluge and rTorrent, Bindery stored a green "path check not required" without checking anything, and the Settings page would not have shown the result even if it had. That is the mechanism behind the most common support report: the connection tests fine, the client accepts grabs, and nothing ever imports. Bindery already knew how to answer the question for NZBGet and rTorrent and simply was not asking. Health is now checked for every client type, shown for every client type, and the three types that genuinely cannot be introspected say so plainly instead of claiming to be fine. Health is also re-checked every 15 minutes, so a client that breaks after setup, a remounted seedbox or a rotated password, no longer stays green until someone presses Test on a hunch.

  **If your Settings page shows a red download client after this update, that is almost certainly a problem that was already there.** Nothing about your setup changed when you updated. What changed is that Bindery now checks, and tells you.
